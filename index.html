About

I like the problems that live between people and systems — why someone hesitates, where a flow loses them, which decision actually matters.

Psychology taught me how people actually think. Business taught me why products live or die. Teaching myself to code means I can build the thing instead of just describing it. Product management is where those three stop being separate subjects.

I work in tight loops — pick a real itch, ship a small version, watch someone use it, iterate. It's how both projects above got built, and it's how I want to work on a product team.
