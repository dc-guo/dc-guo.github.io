About

I like the problems that don't fit on a screen yet — regulated flows, dense data, decisions with real consequences.

My background in industrial design taught me to sweat systems, not just features. I bring that to product: mapping the whole system, finding the one decision that carries the weight, and driving it until the path is obvious.

I work best embedded with design and engineering — prototyping ideas early, owning the roadmap, and shipping in tight loops. Recognized on the Wall of Portfolios with a Silver badge.
